How admission works

  1. Submit the application form.
  2. Verify your email with a six-digit code.
  3. Take the skill assessment (opens in a later release).
  4. Wait for manual review. Passing does not guarantee membership.

Cybersecurity divisions

Pick the division you prefer on the form. It is a preference, not a guaranteed placement.

Authorized testing only

Red Team activity happens only in owned infrastructure or explicitly permitted environments. Membership gives no legal authorization to access anyone else's systems.
